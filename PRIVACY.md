# DuoDeck Privacy Policy

Updated 6 November 2026.

DuoDeck is a fold-aware control surface and remote Mac display client for iPhone and iPad. It is based on Sill, released under Apache License 2.0.

## Data we process
DuoDeck connects to the Sill for Mac companion selected by the user. The Mac's screen frames, audio (if enabled), text and pointer inputs are processed to deliver the remote-control experience. Pairing identifiers, trusted-device information, preferences and selected connection settings are stored on the user's devices. The camera may be accessed only when the user elects to scan a pairing code. Local Network access is used to discover and connect to Macs.

## Where data goes
The application uses encrypted direct connections between the iPhone or iPad and the selected Mac. There is no DuoDeck cloud account, and this fork does not add analytics, advertising SDKs or a hosted relay. If a user routes traffic through a separately configured VPN or third-party network, that provider has its own practices.

## Retention and control
Local preferences and pairing information remain on the device until removed or the app is uninstalled. Removing a paired device on the Mac revokes its access; users can stop sharing by disconnecting. System permissions can be revoked in iOS and macOS Settings.
